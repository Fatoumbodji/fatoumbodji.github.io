Tech Transfer & Strategy
What Does "Building a Bridge" Really Mean in DeepTech?

Building a bridge between research and business goes beyond connecting engineering teams with business units. It means recognizing that the same skills, roles, and practices operate under fundamentally different paradigms in each world.

  • Managing a research team differs from leading a business delivery unit.
  • Collaborating with an academic co-author is not the same as working with a corporate partner.
  • Responding to a peer reviewer requires a different mindset than satisfying a commercial client.
  • Communicating with a scientific audience demands rigour, while communicating with executives demands clarity and impact.
  • University teaching builds knowledge, whereas professional training develops operational practice.

In software engineering, this function mirrors the role of AMOA (Business Analysis & Strategy): understanding technical architecture and business objectives to enable smooth collaboration across teams.

For deeptech ventures and research spin-offs, being a bridge means identifying these differences and adapting existing academic excellence into scalable, market-ready operations.
