Structuring B2B Workflows in Early-Stage Deeptech Projects

October 2026 · By Dr. Fatou Ndiaye MBODJI

In early-stage deeptech projects, a dedicated sales role often comes only after initial market validation. During this interim phase, technical leads and founding teams conduct their own client interactions.

The approach is methodological and operational:

  • Method over Pitching: The goal is not to replace salespeople or to close deals on behalf of the team, but to equip technical teams with the operational methods of B2B sales enablement.
  • Tooling & Infrastructure: Setting up clean CRM pipelines, lead qualification tools, and structured prospecting frameworks.
  • Preparation & Rehearsal: Conducting meeting simulations, drafting discovery questionnaires, and refining meeting structures.

The Outcome: Technical teams gain the structure, methodology, and confidence to lead early B2B meetings effectively until a dedicated sales role is in place.
